Our premium project, Tranquill City, is a premium villa plot development in Rudraram village, Patancheru mandal, Sangareddy district, near Patancheru, Hyderabad. Plot sizes run from 125.42 to 250.84 sq.m (150 to 300 sq.yd), and 78 of the layout's 82 parcels are for sale. Prices are published on the project page. The work is simple to describe: plan the place carefully, share what is known, and give people the space to verify the decision for themselves.

Our trust line for Tranquill City is published plainly: RERA REA01100108192 and HMDA 2103/HMDA/SWDL/2026. Confirm both numbers on the Telangana RERA and HMDA portals. What we do not publish — a possession or handover date, per-band plot counts, drive times — we would rather tell you plainly that the team confirms it than print a figure we cannot stand behind. And the imagery on this site, here and on the project page, is illustrative renders of the planned layout rather than photographs of completed construction; we will not present a render as a photograph of work that has not happened.
