Tranquill City is a gated layout of premium villa plots in Rudraram village, Patancheru mandal, Sangareddy district, near Patancheru in Hyderabad. Plot sizes run from 125.42 to 250.84 sq.m (150 to 300 sq.yd), and the layout holds 82 parcels, four of which are held against HMDA Mortgage conditions. Plots are priced per plot rather than per square metre. The entry price and the price of any specific plot are published on the project page.

The approvals are published, not promised: RERA REA01100108192 and HMDA layout permission 2103/HMDA/SWDL/2026. No possession or handover date is published here, and the project images are renders of the planned layout rather than photographs of completed construction.
