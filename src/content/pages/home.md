Tranquill City is a semi-gated layout of premium villa plots at Mamidipally, near Rudraram and Patancheru, Hyderabad. Plot sizes run from 125.42 to 250.84 sq.m (150 to 300 sq.yd); 70 of the 82 parcels are for sale. HMDA-approved, RERA registered.

The layout is approved — HMDA and RERA — and the approval numbers are published on the project page. No possession or handover date is published, and the project images are renders of the planned layout, not photographs of completed construction. [See the project page](/projects/tranquill-city/) for the full detail.

Buyers want to know what the land costs, whether the paperwork holds up, and what the area is like.
