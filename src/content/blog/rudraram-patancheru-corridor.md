Most plot brochures on the western side of Hyderabad carry the same shorthand: "near Patancheru". If you are searching for **open plots in Rudraram**, **plots for sale in Rudraram**, **villa plots near Patancheru** or **plots near IIT Hyderabad**, the shorthand hides what is actually a long corridor — a highway, an ORR belt, an industrial base and a university campus, most of which a buyer only discovers on the drive out. Two words in that sentence carry more weight than they look, so take them in the sense we use them. **Villa plots** here means land sold for you to build a villa on, not a ready house. **Open plots** is the trade term for land that is available and unbuilt — no fence up, no possession date claimed, no completed structure, as against a plot inside an already-developed township. Ask any two agents in this corridor what they mean by it and you will get two answers.

This is what the corridor contains, and what sits inside Tranquill City on the Rudraram stretch of it.

## NH-65, the Outer Ring Road and the Regional Ring Road: the corridor in three lines

- The **Mumbai Highway (NH-65)** runs through Patancheru and forms the spine of the belt.
- The **Outer Ring Road (ORR)** crosses the same corridor, connecting it to the rest of the city's ring.
- A **Regional Ring Road (RRR)** sits further out. It is not finished: a Telangana government release from August 2024 records the northern works taken up, land acquisition being ordered for the southern stretch that begins at Sangareddy, and the alignment still being revised. That is why land-use conversations in this belt keep referring to it — and why we do not price its effect into anything we publish.

Rudraram sits between Patancheru and the Sangareddy district side of this corridor. Tranquill City's published coordinates are **17.585611°N, 78.148361°E**, and the layout is reached from the NH-65 side.

## Plots near IIT Hyderabad, ICRISAT and the Patancheru industrial belt

**IIT Hyderabad.** One of the country's engineering institutes sits on this corridor, and it is the campus most plot buyers in this belt can name from memory. It is why "plots near IIT Hyderabad" is a search that leads here.

**BHEL Township, Ramachandrapuram.** A public-sector township with its own residential and social infrastructure, one of the reasons the western corridor has a settled population and not only industrial traffic through it.

**ICRISAT.** The international crops research institute near Patancheru — a second long-established anchor on the belt.

**The Patancheru industrial base.** Patancheru and the BDL Township, Isnapur, Kazipally and Pashamylaram are the industrial locations on this stretch. We publish no employment or wage figures for them, for the same reason we publish no drive times: the numbers move, and we would rather say so than print one we cannot stand behind. Ask the team if you want the current picture.

**GITAM University, Rudraram.** A second university campus on this stretch. The university publishes its own location — check it directly rather than taking a broker's description of it.

**The city side.** Miyapur and Lingampally are established residential and metro-connected localities on the western side; Gachibowli and the Financial District continue further along the same arc.

**Everyday infrastructure.** International and CBSE schools and super-specialty hospital facilities serve the belt at Patancheru and Sangareddy. We name the categories and the two towns; we do not publish a school list, a hospital list or a distance, because each of those changes and none of them is ours to fix.

To check the revenue record behind any of these names, the Sangareddy district administration publishes a [village directory](https://sangareddy.telangana.gov.in/villages/), and the Revenue Department's [own record page](https://www.telangana.gov.in/departments/revenue/) is where the department-level services live. Match the village and mandal against those rather than against a brochure.
